What big and small look like

Big is your main camera filling the right third, head in the middle of that third, cropped from the top of your head to the bottom of your ribcage, shoulders running off the right and the bottom. Small is the same feed at about a quarter of the size, bottom right. Video Capture SMALL sits underneath the big camera and stays switched on, so hiding the big camera is all it takes to drop into the corner. Match both against the framing slides in the pre stream deck: centred in frame, even front facing light, green screen behind you.

Optional: switch between big and small with one key

Normally you switch by clicking the eye next to the big camera in the OBS Sources list. That means finding OBS and clicking, in the middle of a lesson. A toggle key does the same job with one press, from wherever you are sitting, and a presenter remote that sends a keystroke works too. This is optional. The eye always works and nothing in the guide depends on you setting this up. It takes about five minutes, and the macOS permissions at the end are the part people miss.

  1. In OBS open Settings, then Hotkeys.
  2. Scroll to your main camera, Video Capture LARGE. It has two boxes: Show and Hide.
  3. Click into the Show box and press the key combination you want. Then click into the Hide box and press the same combination. The same key in both boxes is what makes it a toggle: one press hides the big camera and drops you into the corner, the next press brings it back.
  4. Pick a combination the Mac does not already use. Control, option, shift and a letter is safe. Avoid plain F keys: the top row of a Mac keyboard controls brightness and volume, so F8 on its own is play and pause.
  5. Click Apply, then OK.
  6. Quit OBS completely.
  7. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Accessibility. Press the plus, add OBS, and switch it on.
  8. Still in Privacy and Security, open Input Monitoring. Add OBS there too and switch it on. You need both.
  9. Open OBS again. Neither permission takes effect until OBS restarts.
  10. Test it the way you will use it: bring Canva to the front so OBS is behind, then press the key twice. You should drop into the corner and come back. If it works only when OBS is the front window, one of the two permissions is missing.

Start here: take your copy of the master in Canva

The master is grab and go. You never build in it and you never edit it. Everything below happens in Canva before you go anywhere near OBS.

  1. Open the slide templates in your Canva folder. This is the master.
  2. Pick your method. Either create a new deck and copy in only the slides you need, or duplicate the whole master and delete the slides you do not need. Either way the original master is never touched.
  3. If you are starting a new deck: Create, then Presentation, and give it the lesson name.
  4. Move it into the Studia folder: File, then Move.
  5. Copy the slides you want: right click a slide, Copy, then paste into your new deck.
  6. Type your content into the existing text boxes. Do not add your own boxes: the animations are built into the template ones.
  7. Build the whole lesson block by block against the board below, ticking each block as you finish it.

Your evening, and what each deck page is for

The run up is fixed. 18:00 is the Educreator test with the Live Ops Lead. 18:30 is the test stream, which Ops watch. The go or no go for the evening follows that. 19:00 is the class. The pre stream deck is thirteen pages and each one is checking something specific, so work it in order rather than skipping to the green screen slides.

  1. Pages 1 and 2: the deck is full screen in Present mode and the edge labels are fully visible.
  2. Pages 3 to 5: framing on the big camera, your arm reach, and the small corner camera.
  3. Pages 6 to 9: green screen. See through patches, green edges, ghosting. Chroma Key starts at 430 similarity, 80 smoothness, 100 spill.
  4. Pages 10 to 12: music plays, and the level is balanced against your voice.
  5. Page 13: a test chat message reaches your Chat tab.
  6. Then turn off the Pre Stream Deck eye so the lesson deck shows.

If the test stream will not start from the studio
  1. Make sure Studia is connected to OBS. If you have reloaded the page, press Connect OBS again; it does not reconnect on its own.
  2. Press Start test stream in the studio. This turns on your test key and points OBS at it.
    • If Start test stream says “Fix N issues to start your test stream”: open the Setup panel, use the one-click Fix buttons where offered, or press Start anyway and confirm. Then press Start test stream.
  3. Only if Studia cannot connect to OBS at all (Connect OBS keeps failing), choose Set up OBS by hand, then:
    • In OBS go to Settings → Stream and set Service: Custom and Server: rtmps://global-live.mux.com:443/app.
    • Use the Copy button next to the stream key in the studio and paste it into OBS. Don't retype it.
    • Press Start test stream in the studio first, then Start Streaming in OBS.
  4. For the class itself (manual route only), the test stream and the class use different keys.
    • After you press Prepare broadcast, the studio shows a new key with “Point OBS at your class stream now…”.
    • In OBS, Stop Streaming, paste the new key with the Copy button (the server stays the same), then Start Streaming.
    • Do this straight away. The studio only waits a few minutes for the class stream. If it times out, press Prepare broadcast again and paste the new key it shows.
  5. Go live unlocks once the class stream is received.

Fixes

34 of them. Anything not here goes to the on call person.

Students say the class has not started, but OBS is streaming

Studia is on Recording, not live. Press Go live.

Studia
OBS dropped out right after Prepare broadcast

Expected. Check the mixer straight afterwards.

OBS
Students can see you but cannot hear you

Mic muted in OBS. Unmute in the Audio Mixer.

OBS
Students say the sound is only in one ear

Settings > Audio > Channels is on stereo. Set it to Mono.

OBS
Your voice sounds doubled or echoey

Settings > Audio > Channels is on stereo. Set it to Mono.

OBS
Studia says OBS is not connected

Was OBS open before the tab? Leave OBS running and reload the tab. Otherwise check OBS 28+, WebSocket ticked, password matches, local network prompt allowed. Never Generate Password.

Both
Testing connection never finishes

Same four causes. Reload and start again rather than waiting.

Studia
The main button in Studia does nothing

OBS is not connected. The first step is the grey Connect OBS button.

Studia
The studio will not work in Safari

Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Browser
Studia is asking you to pair again

Pairing is stored per browser. A new laptop, browser or cleared profile means pairing again.

Browser
OBS says it is streaming but nothing arrives in Studia

OBS was never connected, so Settings > Stream holds the old destination. Stop, connect, set the stream up again from Studia.

OBS
You cannot find the Test stream tick box

Right of the Level row on Create your live class. It must be ticked before Next.

Studia
Can students see my test class

No. Not students, not parents, not the public calendar.

Studia
You forgot to tick Test stream

End the class immediately, tell the on call person, create a fresh one with the box ticked.

Studia
OBS is pointed somewhere unexpected

Studia writes the stream settings into whichever OBS profile is open when you connect. If you changed profile, set the stream up again from Studia.

OBS
Settings reset themselves after an OBS update

An auto update can reset the camera and deck source to 800 x 600. Re check the three resolutions.

OBS
The go small toggle key does nothing

Video Capture SMALL's eye is closed, or you are in the wrong scene collection. If you bound a toggle key and it only works while OBS is the front window, OBS is missing Accessibility or Input Monitoring in System Settings, Privacy and Security. Add both and restart OBS. The eye on the big camera always works.

OBS
You are permanently small

Video Capture LARGE's eye is clicked off, so the big shot never appears. Click the eye beside Video Capture LARGE back on.

OBS
Green screen shows behind you when you go small

Video Capture SMALL has no Chroma Key filter on it. The filter is per source, so adding it to the big camera does nothing for the small camera. Right click Video Capture SMALL > Filters > add Chroma Key, and match the same values.

OBS
The key behaves oddly after an OBS update

An old version 1 Chroma Key filter reads differently from the current one. Delete it and add a fresh Chroma Key.

OBS
Green fringe, or parts of you cut out

Fringe: Similarity down. Cut out: Smoothness up. Green on skin: Spill up and move further from the screen.

OBS
Similarity has crept past 700

A lighting fault. Softbox at least 120 cm off the screen, you about 60 cm in front, fabric flat, every other light in the room off, then reset to 430 / 80 / 100.

Room
Your corner cut out looks soft

Video Capture SMALL is capturing below 1920 x 1080. Set its resolution in Properties.

OBS
The deck looks soft or blurry

Check all three typed resolutions first. If those are right, the deck source is not filling the canvas: drag it out to the edges.

OBS
Slide layer is black

Two causes. Either the eye icon beside the deck source in OBS is clicked off, so the layer is hidden: click the eye to switch it back on. Or the Canva link is not set to anyone with the link can view, or the URL is wrong.

Both
Deck jumps back to slide 1

Untick Refresh browser when scene becomes active, and Shutdown source when not visible.

OBS
Arrow keys not advancing slides

The Interact window lost focus. Click inside it.

OBS
No music at all

Click once inside Interact to unlock the audio, then check Canva's own volume.

OBS
You can hear the music, students cannot

Advanced Audio Properties > the deck source > Audio Monitoring > Monitor and Output.

OBS
Music on the stream but you cannot hear it

Monitoring is off, or the Monitoring Device is not Default.

OBS
Music drowning out your voice

Set the music to volume 2 in Canva. That is the level the template is built around. Leave the deck fader in OBS where it is.

Canva
Stream freezes or stutters

Apple VT Hardware, 4000, 30 fps, Ethernet. Quit everything else. Then stop the stream, Help > Log Files > Upload Current Log File and send the link.

OBS
Camera or canvas is black

System Settings > Privacy and Security > Camera and Screen Recording, switch OBS on, restart OBS.

Mac
You cannot find the class page

Studia does not link to it yet. Bookmark it, or ask the on call person once.
